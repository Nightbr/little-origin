# Partner Collaboration

Little Origin is built for two (or more) people choosing a name together.

## Members

Each person gets their own account — created during onboarding or later from the **Members** page.

<p>
  <img src={require('@site/static/img/screenshots/members.png').default} alt="Members page listing existing members and a form to add a new one" width="300" />
</p>

## How It Works

- **Same names, shared preferences** — everyone swipes through the same pool, filtered by shared settings
- **Private swipes** — your likes and passes are your own
- **Shared matches** — only mutual likes are revealed, instantly, to everyone

## Privacy

Matches and their like-counts are visible to all members. Your individual swipe history and credentials are yours alone.
